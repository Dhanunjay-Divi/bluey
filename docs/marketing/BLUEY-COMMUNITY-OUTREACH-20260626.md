# Bluey Community Outreach - 2026-06-26

## Outreach Posture

Bluey should be shared as a real workflow tool, not as a spammy AI launch. Lead with the problem, show a concrete use case, and ask for feedback from people who spend time in technical meetings.

## Good Communities

- Devtools founder groups.
- Engineering leadership communities.
- Indie software communities.
- AI productivity communities.
- Support, solutions engineering, and technical customer success groups.
- Local founder Slack or Discord groups where the owner is already a member.

## Communities To Handle Carefully

- Hacker News: use a technical, transparent write-up and expect hard questions.
- Reddit: follow subreddit rules, avoid drive-by promotion, and ask moderators when unsure.
- LinkedIn: practical product story and workflow clips work better than hype.
- X: short demo clips, build logs, and customer-problem posts.

## First Outreach Message

Subject: Quick feedback on an AI copilot for engineering meetings?

I am building Bluey, a desktop AI copilot that answers from live meeting context: transcript, screen, docs, code, files, and saved project memory. It is meant for design reviews, incident calls, planning, and customer debugging where a normal chatbot misses the context.

Would you be open to trying the alpha or giving feedback on the workflow?

## Follow-Up Questions

- What meeting or work session would you actually use this in?
- What context would you expect it to read?
- What answer format would help most: short overlay, detailed canvas, bullet plan, or code patch?
- What privacy or security question would stop you from adopting it?
- What would make the credit model feel clear?

## Owner-Only Actions

- Public posts from owner accounts.
- Community moderator requests.
- Founder story and personal demos.
- Customer quotes, only after explicit permission.
