# Bluey Launch Calendar - 2026-06-26

## Week 0 - Deploy And Verify

- Deploy `web/` to `bluey.sh`.
- Confirm `/llms.txt`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/how-bluey-works/`, `/bluey-faq/`, and high-intent pages are live.
- Run install smoke tests for macOS and Windows links.
- Confirm account, download, reload, and login flows still work.
- Capture fresh screenshots and a short screen recording.

## Week 1 - Search And Soft Launch

- Submit sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Import or verify in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit sitemap.
- Request indexing for homepage, how-it-works, FAQ, and top feature pages.
- Send direct outreach to 25 high-fit users.
- Publish one founder note and one technical workflow post.

## Week 2 - Community Feedback

- Post a feedback-first thread in one founder/devtools community.
- Add answers from real user questions to FAQ.
- Publish a comparison note: Bluey vs meeting notetakers.
- Review activation metrics: install, account creation, first answer, first reload.

## Week 3 - Expansion

- Publish a screen-context workflow page or video.
- Publish a design-review workflow page or video.
- Reach out to 10 teams with engineering-heavy meetings.
- Check Google and Bing indexing coverage.

## Week 4 - Launch Push

- Product Hunt or similar launch only after install/reload/onboarding are stable.
- Post to LinkedIn, X, HN, and Reddit with tailored copy.
- Monitor support, billing, usage, and provider-cost metrics daily.
- Summarize outcomes in the next numbered Bluey round doc.
