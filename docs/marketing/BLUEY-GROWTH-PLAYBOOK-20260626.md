# Bluey Growth Playbook - 2026-06-26

## Positioning

Bluey is a private desktop AI copilot for live technical work. The sharpest message is not "another meeting bot" or "another coding agent." It is:

> Ask from the context already around your engineering meeting: live conversation, screen, files, code, docs, and saved project memory.

## Ideal Users

- Founders and technical operators who live in calls, docs, code, and customer context.
- Engineers who need source-aware help during design reviews, implementation discussions, incidents, and planning.
- Support and solutions teams who need live context from a customer call and internal notes.
- Product teams that move between requirements, screenshots, roadmap decisions, and technical tradeoffs.

## Messaging Pillars

- Live context: Bluey answers while work is happening.
- Source-aware: transcript, screen, files, code, docs, saved memory, and managed sources stay distinct.
- Private by default: visible controls, account-scoped data, and clear capture boundaries.
- Cost visible: start free, then use credits for paid AI, speech, screen analysis, and saved-session search.
- Compact overlay, fuller canvas: short answers stay close to work; deeper answers can open in a larger detail surface.

## Channels

- Search: high-intent pages now exist for context copilot, engineering meetings, screen context, saved memory, router, overlay, and comparisons.
- Community: founder, engineering, AI tooling, dev productivity, and indie software communities.
- Direct sales: technical founders, devtools teams, agencies, support engineers, and product teams.
- Content: short workflows, screenshots, "before Bluey / after Bluey" examples, and comparison pages.

## Launch Sequence

1. Deploy the static site pack and verify live pages.
2. Submit sitemap and priority URLs to Google and Bing.
3. Publish a calm founder-style launch note with a screen recording.
4. Post in one or two relevant communities with a real story, not a drive-by link.
5. Reach out to 25 high-fit users with a specific workflow example.
6. Monitor install, account, reload, and answer-success metrics before broad posting.

## Guardrails

- Do not market Bluey as a way to bypass rules, exams, proctoring, platform controls, consent, or security.
- Do not imply capture is invisible or guaranteed to avoid every recorder.
- Do not show sensitive customer content in public screenshots.
- Do not claim web search is generally available until the managed server-side retrieval lane is deployed and monitored.

## Weekly Growth Rhythm

- Monday: review analytics, search impressions, install funnel, and activation.
- Tuesday: publish one workflow page or short post.
- Wednesday: send direct outreach to a narrow persona.
- Thursday: gather feedback from alpha users and update FAQ.
- Friday: ship one product or landing improvement tied to user confusion.
