Comparison

Bluey helps while the work is happening.

Meeting notetakers are useful for records and summaries. Bluey is focused on live help: answering from transcript, screen, files, code, docs, and saved memory when the team needs a response now.

Key differences

Meeting notetaker

Usually joins a call, records, transcribes, summarizes, and creates a meeting artifact afterward.

Bluey

Runs as a desktop copilot and answers from the current work context during the meeting or work session.

Best together

Use notes for the record and Bluey for live reasoning, source checks, follow-ups, and technical answers.

Where Bluey fits

Bluey is useful when the question depends on more than the transcript: a shared screen, attached docs, code, a saved decision, or an external source that needs verification.
