Privacy Policy

Bluey is an AI work assistant that runs a desktop overlay and optional cloud services. We collect only what is needed for account login, credit reloads, AI answers, saved sessions, support, and abuse prevention.

Information We Process

  • Account data such as email, password hash, device/session tokens, balance, reload history, and usage metadata.
  • User-approved context such as typed prompts, source-labeled transcripts, attached files, screen-analysis results, answer-style instructions, and generated responses.
  • Operational data such as trace IDs, error logs, provider health, IP-derived abuse controls, and support bundles. Logs are designed to avoid secrets and raw provider keys.
  • Payment checkout metadata from Square. Bluey does not collect, store, or proxy card numbers, CVV, or card expiration data.

How We Use Data

We use account and session data to authenticate you, answer requests, meter paid usage, save approved context, provide searchable history, show balance and cost labels, debug failures, prevent abuse, and support export or deletion requests.

AI Providers and Context

When a request needs an AI model, Bluey sends only the context needed for that request: your prompt, selected transcript snippets, attached document text, screen-analysis output, saved session memory, and routing metadata such as task type and cost estimate. Speech transcription, image understanding, embedding, and answer generation may use separate providers.

Files, Sessions, and Memory

Documents, images, screenshots, and other files you attach are parsed into readable text where supported and are tied to the active Bluey session. When you sign in with saved-session sync on, Bluey may store the original synced file bytes, lightweight previews, extracted text, summaries, and search indexes so another signed-in device can restore the session and answer from the same context. You can turn cloud sync off in settings and keep using local sessions.

Your Controls

Desktop capture starts only through visible user controls. You can stop listening, hide the overlay, attach or remove documents, export account data, delete your account, and clear local state.

Retention

Account-credit batches remain in the ledger for accounting and expire up to 12 months (365 days) after purchase. Cloud-synced session records, transcripts, document bytes, screenshots, extracted text, summaries, and search indexes are kept for up to 12 months (365 days) from their last sync or use unless you delete them earlier. Cleanup is lazy, so expired objects may remain briefly until a sweeper, restore attempt, or maintenance pass removes them, but they are no longer intended for active product use.

Account Deletion

Deleting your account is permanent. It removes account records, local-device authorization records, saved cloud sessions, synced files, screenshots, transcripts, extracted text, generated answers, usage history, and related searchable indexes where deletion is technically available. Unused Bluey credits are lost when the account is deleted, so contact support before deletion if you need a billing review.

Security

Desktop account tokens are stored in Bluey's private local account profile so the app can reconnect without operating-system keychain prompts. Provider API keys stay server-side for managed cloud requests. Logs are designed for debugging and support, not for storing raw secrets or full user content.

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure, payment, email, and AI providers only to operate Bluey. We do not sell customer data. Provider usage is governed by Bluey-managed accounts and server-side policies.

Contact

For privacy, security, export, deletion, or billing questions, contact support at the address published in the product account page.

Terms of Use

These terms cover the Bluey alpha service. By creating an account, installing Bluey, or using Bluey Cloud, you agree to use the product responsibly and only where you have the right to capture, upload, or analyze the context you provide.

Account Credits

  • Bluey is not a monthly subscription by default. You reload non-transferable account credits and spend them on AI model requests, speech transcription, screen analysis, searchable history, and related cloud work.
  • Credits have no cash value, are not a stored-value card or gift card, are not transferable, and cannot be used outside Bluey.
  • Credits are valid for up to 12 months (365 days) from purchase. Bluey spends the oldest unexpired credit batch first and stops at zero so you do not accrue usage debt.
  • Deleting your account forfeits unused credits. Contact support before deleting if you want a billing review for unused credits.
  • Refund handling, payment disputes, taxes, and receipts follow the support and payment-provider process shown in your account page.

Acceptable Use

You may use Bluey for your own work, meetings, documents, coding, research, and day-to-day productivity. You may not use Bluey to break laws, access data you are not authorized to access, deceive third-party assessment/security systems, harvest credentials or session cookies, or bypass platform restrictions.

User-Controlled Context

You are responsible for the documents, transcripts, screenshots, repositories, and other context you attach or ask Bluey to analyze. Bluey should be used with consent where required, and it should not be used for hidden capture of people or information you are not allowed to process.

Cloud Sync and Retention

If you sign in with cloud sync or saved sessions enabled, you authorize Bluey to store synced session metadata, transcripts, generated answers, original file or screenshot bytes, extracted text, summaries, and search indexes for restoration and answer context. These synced objects are retained for up to 12 months (365 days) from last sync or use unless you delete them earlier. Expired objects are removed lazily during cleanup, restore attempts, or maintenance passes.

AI Output

Bluey can help draft answers, summarize sessions, explain code, reason about systems, and organize context. AI output may be incomplete or wrong. You are responsible for reviewing generated output before relying on it, sending it, submitting it, or using it to make decisions.

Payments

Square or another checkout provider may process reloads, receipts, disputes, and payment methods. Bluey records enough checkout metadata to credit your account, prevent duplicate crediting, show usage, and support refunds or account deletion.

Service Availability

Bluey is an alpha product. AI providers, speech transcription, billing providers, networks, and local OS permissions can fail or degrade. We work to fail clearly with logs and status, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted service.

Changes

We may update these terms as Bluey moves from alpha to broader release. Material changes should be reflected on this page and in release notes.

Product note for the macOS alpha

Low-profile controls

Bluey's desktop controls are designed to stay out of your way while you work. The overlay can hide back to a compact pill, use capture-excluded windows where the operating system supports it, and keep session controls close to the screen context you are using.

What the controls do

  • Pill: the compact Bluey launcher. Click it to open the current session window; drag it to move it.
  • Hide: collapses the expanded window back to the pill. Bluey keeps running until you turn it off.
  • Turn off: stops the daemon and closes the overlay. Run bluey on to start again.
  • Capture-excluded windows: normal OS screen recording and screen-share APIs should not include the overlay. Privileged tools, managed devices, cameras, or custom capture systems may still observe the screen.

Responsible use

Bluey is for user-approved work context: meetings, documents, code, research, and day-to-day productivity. Do not use it to access data you are not authorized to access or to bypass third-party rules. If a workflow requires consent, get consent before capturing or uploading context.

Why this matters

The product goal is calm focus, not deception. You should always know when Bluey is listening, what context is attached, and whether an answer used an AI model, screen analysis, or saved context.
