Privacy Policy

Bluey is a consent-first AI work assistant with a desktop overlay and optional cloud services. Listening, screen analysis, file attachment, cloud sync, and automatic payments are separate choices. Bluey processes only the context needed for the features you choose.

Information We Process

  • Account data such as email, password hash, device/session tokens, balance, reload history, and usage records.
  • User-approved context such as typed prompts, source-labeled transcripts, temporary audio chunks needed for transcription, attached files, screen-analysis results, answer-style instructions, and generated responses.
  • Diagnostic data such as error reports, service health, abuse controls, and support bundles. Logs are designed to avoid secrets and full user content.
  • Payment information needed to complete reloads. Bluey does not collect or store full card numbers, CVV, or card expiration data.
  • Bluey Jobs data such as your Career Profile, verified application email addresses and labels, resume versions, work and education history, skills, location and salary preferences, job matches, application answers, browser-run events, interventions, submission screenshots, and receipts.

How We Use Data

We use account and session data to authenticate you, answer requests, meter paid usage, provide the local or cloud history you choose, show balance and cost labels, debug failures, prevent abuse, and support export or deletion requests.

Security and Abuse Signals

To protect accounts, product content, and service availability, Bluey may process request metadata such as IP address, user agent, trusted proxy headers, TLS or browser fingerprints where available, account and device identifiers, authentication outcomes, request paths, response sizes, error rates, and traversal velocity. We use these signals to rate-limit abusive traffic, detect automated extraction or account farming, investigate security events, and enforce access restrictions. Ordinary security logs are designed not to contain resume text, prompts, transcripts, application answers, cookies, passwords, payment details, or verification codes.

Training and Product Improvement

Bluey does not use your prompts, transcripts, audio, files, screenshots, or generated answers to train or fine-tune models. A future research or training program would require a separate, clearly labeled opt-in. The current product has no such opt-in, so submitted content is not training data. We may use aggregate service metrics and feedback you deliberately submit to diagnose reliability and improve safeguards.

AI, Speech, and Context

When a request needs AI or speech transcription, Bluey sends only the context needed for that request: your prompt, selected transcript snippets, relevant temporary audio chunks, attached document text, screen-analysis output, enabled saved-session memory, and request details such as task type and approximate cost. Bluey may route different requests through different answer, speech, image, or search services depending on the task and selected context.

Cloud Sync, Files, and Memory

Cloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in desktop settings, Bluey may upload the session records, transcripts, answers, attached file or screenshot bytes, extracted text, summaries, and search indexes needed to restore that approved context on another signed-in device. Turning sync off stops new automatic uploads; local sessions remain available on that device.

Audio Retention

Bluey does not offer a retained-audio library in the current release. Raw audio is processed transiently for transcription and Bluey's temporary local chunks are deleted after transcription. Transcript text may remain in the local session and may sync only when cloud sync is on. Speech providers process audio needed to return the transcript under their service terms.

Bluey Jobs

When you use Bluey Jobs, we process the profile facts, preferences, job descriptions, tailored documents, application answers, and status records needed to find roles, prepare job-specific materials, and track applications. When you approve or enable submission, Bluey sends the selected resume, cover letter, answers, and contact details to that employer and the application provider operating its form.

If you choose to remember an application answer, Bluey stores it in your encrypted Answer Memory and reuses it according to the scope you select: one company, one Career Track, or all applications. You can review, edit, or remove saved answers in Jobs settings. Sensitive questions may still pause for your direct review.

For each submitted application, Bluey keeps a receipt identifying the exact resume version and files used, the submission confirmation, and the application answers sent. When an email or calendar connection is available and enabled for your account, related status records may also be linked to that application. This lets you review what was sent without mixing records between employers or roles.

A local Bluey Browser keeps its job-site profile on your computer. Cloud runs may store an encrypted browser profile and cookies so an application you authorized can continue while your computer is off. You can remove Bluey-controlled browser profiles from Jobs settings. Bluey does not place raw job-site passwords in ordinary logs or application records.

You can verify more than one application email and choose a default or a different address for each Career Track. Bluey records which verified address was used with each application. If an email or calendar connection is available to your account and you enable it, Bluey may process the mailbox or calendar identifier, provider authorization, aliases, and application or interview status signals shown in that feature. During an application, Bluey may identify a matching, recent email verification code and ask you to approve using it. The code is handled transiently and is not retained in the application timeline or ordinary logs. Addresses delivered into the same mailbox may appear as aliases of one connection. You can remove an application email or disconnect a provider from Jobs settings. Job sites and connected providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Your Controls

Desktop capture starts only through visible user controls. You can stop listening, remove attached context, turn cloud sync off, keep sessions local, export account data, delete your account, and clear local state. Auto Reload is also off until you turn it on and approve a saved card.

Retention

Purchased balance remains in account records for accounting and expires up to 12 months (365 days) after purchase. Bluey does not retain raw audio after transcription by default. Local session records remain on your device until you remove them. The current cloud product does not yet guarantee automatic deletion after a fixed number of days; synced records may remain until you delete the session or account. Contact support for a deletion review if needed.

Bluey Jobs keeps your Career Profile, verified application emails, preferences, tailored documents, application answers, receipts, and outcome history while your account is active so you can reuse facts and review prior applications. You can remove individual Jobs records where that control is available or delete the account. Connected-service access ends when you disconnect it, and Bluey-controlled cloud browser profiles are removed when you remove them or delete the account, subject to limited backup and legal retention periods.

Account Deletion

Deleting your account is permanent and requests removal of account access, device authorizations, account-scoped synced content, Jobs profiles, tailored resumes, application records, and Bluey-controlled browser profiles. Payment, fraud-prevention, security, or backup records may be retained where required, and deletion across storage systems may not be immediate. The current product does not yet show per-object deletion status. Unused account balance is lost, so contact support before deletion if you need a billing or deletion review.

Security

Desktop account tokens are stored in Bluey's private local account profile so the app can reconnect without operating-system keychain prompts. Payment and AI credentials are kept outside the desktop app. Logs are designed for debugging and support, not for storing raw secrets or full user content.

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure, payment, email, calendar, speech, AI, and application-processing services only to operate the features you request. For Bluey Jobs, an employer and its application provider receive only the application materials you choose to submit. Bundled open-source components do not receive your data merely because Bluey uses them. We do not sell customer data, and submitted content is not used for model training.

Contact

For privacy, security, export, deletion, or billing questions, contact support at the address published in the product account page.

Desktop privacy note

Screen-share privacy

Bluey's overlay stays visible to you while you work. On supported desktop paths, the operating system is asked to exclude the overlay from ordinary screen recordings and screen shares. This is a user-controlled privacy convenience, not an invisibility guarantee or a way to bypass monitoring.

What the controls do

  • Visible state: the Bluey pill and status indicator show when the product is running. Listening and screen analysis have their own visible controls.
  • Show or hide: collapses or restores the overlay for your own workspace. Hiding the window does not imply that capture, transcription, or background work has stopped.
  • Turn off: stops the daemon and closes the overlay. Run bluey on to start again.
  • Capture exclusion: ordinary OS capture APIs may omit the overlay where supported. Meeting apps, privileged tools, managed devices, cameras, or custom capture systems may still show it. Test your sharing setup before relying on this behavior.

Responsible use

Bluey is for user-approved work context: meetings, documents, code, research, and day-to-day productivity. Do not use it to access data you are not authorized to access, impersonate another application, or bypass third-party rules. If a workflow requires consent, get consent before listening, capturing, or uploading context.

Why this matters

The product goal is calm focus with explicit controls. Bluey does not claim to be covert or undetectable. You should always know when it is listening, what context is attached, and whether an answer used screen analysis, files, transcript, or saved context.

1

Open Terminal and run:
