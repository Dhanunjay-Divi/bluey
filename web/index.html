Privacy Policy

Bluey is an AI work assistant that runs a desktop overlay and optional cloud services. We collect only what is needed for account login, balance reloads, AI answers, saved sessions, support, and abuse prevention.

Information We Process

  • Account data such as email, password hash, device/session tokens, balance, reload history, and usage records.
  • User-approved context such as typed prompts, source-labeled transcripts, voice/audio recordings or chunks, attached files, screen-analysis results, answer-style instructions, and generated responses.
  • Diagnostic data such as error reports, service health, abuse controls, and support bundles. Logs are designed to avoid secrets and full user content.
  • Payment information needed to complete reloads. Bluey does not collect or store full card numbers, CVV, or card expiration data.

How We Use Data

We use account and session data to authenticate you, answer requests, meter paid usage, save approved context, provide searchable history, show balance and cost labels, debug failures, prevent abuse, and support export or deletion requests.

Training and Product Improvement

When you use cloud sync, saved sessions, speech, screen, or file features, Bluey may use prompts, transcripts, voice/audio recordings or chunks, attached context, screen-analysis results, generated responses, feedback, and usage patterns to evaluate transcription and answer quality, train or tune Bluey systems, improve routing, debug product behavior, and build better safeguards. Do not submit confidential, regulated, or third-party content unless you have permission for that use.

AI, Speech, and Context

When a request needs AI or speech transcription, Bluey sends the context needed for that request: your prompt, selected transcript snippets, relevant audio chunks, attached document text, screen-analysis output, saved session memory, and request details such as task type and approximate cost. Bluey may route different requests through different answer, speech, image, or search paths depending on the task and selected context.

Files, Sessions, and Memory

Documents, images, screenshots, and other files you attach are parsed into readable text where supported and are tied to the active Bluey session. When you sign in with saved-session sync on, Bluey may store the original synced file bytes, lightweight previews, extracted text, summaries, and search indexes so another signed-in device can restore the session and answer from the same context. You can turn cloud sync off in settings and keep using local sessions.

Your Controls

Desktop capture starts only through visible user controls. You can stop listening, hide the overlay, attach or remove documents, export account data, delete your account, and clear local state.

Retention

Purchased balance remains in account records for accounting and expires up to 12 months (365 days) after purchase. Cloud-synced session records, transcripts, voice/audio recordings or chunks, document bytes, screenshots, extracted text, summaries, generated responses, and search indexes are kept for up to 90 days from their last sync or use unless you delete them earlier. Cleanup is lazy, so expired objects may remain briefly until a maintenance pass removes them, but they are no longer intended for active product use.

Account Deletion

Deleting your account is permanent. It removes account records, local-device authorization records, saved cloud sessions, synced files, screenshots, transcripts, voice/audio recordings or chunks, extracted text, generated answers, usage history, and related searchable indexes where deletion is technically available. Unused account balance is lost when the account is deleted, so contact support before deletion if you need a billing review.

Security

Desktop account tokens are stored in Bluey's private local account profile so the app can reconnect without operating-system keychain prompts. Payment and AI credentials are kept outside the desktop app. Logs are designed for debugging and support, not for storing raw secrets or full user content.

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure, payment, email, and AI services only to operate Bluey. We do not sell customer data. AI usage is governed by Bluey-managed accounts and product policies.

Contact

For privacy, security, export, deletion, or billing questions, contact support at the address published in the product account page.

Terms of Use

These terms cover the Bluey service. By creating an account, installing Bluey, or using Bluey Cloud, you agree to use the product responsibly and only where you have the right to capture, upload, or analyze the context you provide.

Account Balance

  • Bluey is not a monthly subscription by default. You add prepaid account balance and spend it on AI model requests, speech transcription, screen analysis, searchable history, and related cloud work.
  • Usage estimates are approximate. Auto routing may choose a lighter or deeper path based on the task, transcript, screen, files, saved context, and answer depth.
  • Balance has no cash value, is not a stored-value card or gift card, is not transferable, and cannot be used outside Bluey.
  • Balance is valid for up to 12 months (365 days) from purchase. Bluey spends the oldest unexpired balance first and stops at zero so you do not accrue usage debt.
  • Deleting your account forfeits unused balance. Contact support before deleting if you want a billing review.
  • Refund handling, payment disputes, taxes, and receipts follow the billing support process shown in your account page.

Acceptable Use

You may use Bluey for your own work, meetings, documents, coding, research, and day-to-day productivity. You may not use Bluey to break laws, access data you are not authorized to access, deceive third-party assessment/security systems, harvest credentials or session cookies, or bypass platform restrictions.

User-Controlled Context

You are responsible for the documents, transcripts, voice/audio, screenshots, repositories, and other context you attach or ask Bluey to analyze. Bluey should be used with consent where required, and it should not be used for hidden capture of people or information you are not allowed to process.

Training and Product Improvement

When you submit or sync context, you give Bluey permission to host, process, copy, analyze, evaluate, and use submitted context, voice/audio recordings or chunks, transcripts, and generated responses to provide the service, improve transcription and answer quality, train or tune Bluey systems, improve routing, and build safeguards. You must only submit content that you have the right to provide for those purposes.

Cloud Sync and Retention

If you sign in with cloud sync or saved sessions enabled, you authorize Bluey to store synced session details, transcripts, voice/audio recordings or chunks, generated answers, original file or screenshot bytes, extracted text, summaries, and search indexes for restoration, answer context, transcription review, and the product-improvement uses described above. These synced objects are retained for up to 90 days from last sync or use unless you delete them earlier. Expired objects are removed lazily during cleanup, restore attempts, or maintenance passes.

AI Output

Bluey can help draft answers, summarize sessions, explain code, reason about systems, and organize context. AI output may be incomplete or wrong. You are responsible for reviewing generated output before relying on it, sending it, submitting it, or using it to make decisions.

Payments

Payments, receipts, disputes, and saved cards are managed through checkout. Bluey keeps what is needed to update your balance, show usage, and support refunds or account deletion.

Service Availability

AI, speech transcription, billing, networks, and local OS permissions can fail or degrade. We work to fail clearly with status and support details, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted service.

Changes

We may update these terms as Bluey changes. Material changes should be reflected on this page and in release notes.

Product note for macOS

Low-profile controls

Bluey's desktop controls are designed to stay out of your way while you work. The overlay can hide back to a compact pill, use capture-excluded windows where the operating system supports it, and keep session controls close to the screen context you are using.

What the controls do

  • Pill: the compact Bluey launcher. Click it to open the current session window; drag it to move it.
  • Hide: collapses the expanded window back to the pill. Bluey keeps running until you turn it off.
  • Turn off: stops the daemon and closes the overlay. Run bluey on to start again.
  • Capture-excluded windows: normal OS screen recording and screen-share APIs should not include the overlay. Privileged tools, managed devices, cameras, or custom capture systems may still observe the screen.

Responsible use

Bluey is for user-approved work context: meetings, documents, code, research, and day-to-day productivity. Do not use it to access data you are not authorized to access or to bypass third-party rules. If a workflow requires consent, get consent before capturing or uploading context.

Why this matters

The product goal is calm focus, not deception. You should always know when Bluey is listening, what context is attached, and whether an answer used an AI model, screen analysis, or saved context.

Terminal setup

Copy these two commands.

1

Open Terminal and run:

curl -fsSL https://bluey.sh/install.sh | bash
2

Start the desktop app:

bluey on
If a code appears, sign in here and press Connect.
Install taking longer than 5 minutes, showing any error, or Bluey not working? Run bluey support and email the generated support bundle to hello@bluey.sh. If that fails, run bluey doctor to check permissions or bluey doctor --zip to create the same support bundle.

Overlay shortcuts

Use these while Bluey is open. On Mac the global modifier is Ctrl+Option; on Windows it is Ctrl+Alt.

CtrlOptionB CtrlAltB Minimize to pill or restore
CtrlOptionT CtrlAltT Focus Ask text input
CtrlOptionL CtrlAltL Start or stop Listen
CtrlOptionS CtrlAltS Capture screen context
CtrlOptionI CtrlAltI Toggle click-through
CtrlOptionEnter CtrlAltEnter Answer now
Esc Close the active panel
TabShiftTab Move between Bluey controls
OptionUpDown AltUpDown Scroll answers, canvas, or history
Click-through off: blank Bluey space drags the window; mouse wheel scrolls the content under the pointer. Click-through on: blank clicks pass through; use global shortcuts and Bluey scroll zones.
